🧪 Experimental beta: use with caution.

What's new in 2.70 beta 3
- New **transparent car** (test): on the 360° view and in 3D, the road the cameras saw a moment earlier appears under the car, which becomes see-through little by little. It is a memory, not a live image: never rely on this view alone. Button on the 360° view (third at top right) or Settings › 360° view › Car, with the "i" explaining it. It works only up to 7 km/h; it comes back below 5.
- Optional, anonymous sending of the diagnostic report (Settings › Diagnostics, off by default). No position, photos, videos, number plate, VIN or personal data are sent; the "i" explains what is sent.
- Fix: if the app opened by itself (reverse or turn signal), the wheel view button no longer keeps it open in P or N.
