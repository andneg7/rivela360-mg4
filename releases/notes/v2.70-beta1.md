🧪 Experimental beta: use with caution.

What's new in 2.70 beta 1
- New "Panoramic" camera view: straighter in the middle and wide at the edges, with no black corners and the front bumper in sight. You choose it for each camera: Panoramic (default) or Original.
- Sharper camera image.
- 3D view: three wheel choices, X-Power (default, with orange brake calipers), Luxury/Trophy 18″ and Luxury MY22 17″ with 215/50 R17 tyres. New slider for the model's light and brighter rear lights.
- 3D view: path lines and parking sensors become 70 cm see-through curtains, green, yellow or red depending on the distance.
- Wheel view: four windows beside the camera, each with its X; one tap enlarges a window. It also works when driving forward with the front camera and opens by itself when parallel parking.
- Dynamic lines in two styles, Classic and Modern, with all-white marks at 1, 2 and 3 m; the "i" explains the distances.
- When opened from its icon, the app can stay open until you close it.
- Videos without the photo and record buttons: to stop recording, tap the right side of the screen.
- More stable: selecting reverse closes the settings and any open windows; safer recordings; fewer stutters while manoeuvring.
- Animated explanations in the settings ("i" button) and a new app icon.
