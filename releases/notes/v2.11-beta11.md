🧪 Experimental beta: use with caution.

What's new in beta 11
- Works together with MG4-360-Camera-App by jamakr4: when Rivela opens the cameras it asks the other app to release them, and gives them back when it closes (requires the MG4-360-Camera-App version with this feature). If Rivela closes unexpectedly, the cameras go back to the other app after a few seconds, never in reverse.
- Wheel view: if you switch it off by hand it stays off for the whole manoeuvre (until 12 km/h or P). Before, it switched back on by itself after a few metres.
- Rivela 360 logo at the top of the credits.
- New "What's new" window: after each update it shows once what has changed. You can read it again in Settings › Updates.

Read the warnings before installing: [DISCLAIMER.en.md](https://github.com/andneg7/rivela360-mg4/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.en.md)
